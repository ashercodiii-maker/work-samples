# The Latte Myth: Why Skipping Coffee Won't Make You Rich — Long-form, ~8 min

**[COLD OPEN — 0:00]**
You've heard it a hundred times: skip the daily latte and you'll retire a millionaire. Let's actually do the math, because the real number is going to surprise you, and not in the way you think.

**[PROMISE — 0:15]**
By the end of this video you'll know the three money decisions that matter more than every coffee you'll ever buy combined, and the one situation where the latte advice is actually right.

**[SECTION 1 — The math, done honestly — 0:40]**
- $5 a day is about $1,825 a year.
- Invested monthly for 30 years at an assumed 7% average annual return, that grows to roughly $185,000.
- *Open loop:* That sounds huge. But hold that number, because in section 3 we'll compare it to something most people never think about.

**[SECTION 2 — Why people quit the latte plan — 2:30]**
- Small, daily sacrifices are hard to sustain; motivation fades.
- Saving $5 only works if the $5 actually gets invested, automatically.
- *Pattern interrupt:* Quick poll on screen: "Have you ever tried a no-spend challenge? How long did it last?"

**[SECTION 3 — The big three — 4:00]** *(payoff for the open loop)*
1. **Housing:** a rent or mortgage difference of $300/month is 5x the latte.
2. **Transportation:** the car you finance often costs more than a decade of coffee.
3. **Income:** one successful raise negotiation can beat 30 years of skipped lattes.

**[SECTION 4 — When the latte advice IS right — 6:30]**
If you're carrying credit card debt at 20%+, every small cut matters, because you're paying interest on the latte too.

**[OUTRO + CTA — 7:30]**
Enjoy the coffee. Fight the big three. If you want the 10-minute checklist for negotiating a raise, that's the next video. It's on screen now.
