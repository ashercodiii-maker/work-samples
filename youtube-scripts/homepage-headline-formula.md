# The 3-Line Homepage Formula - Long-form, ~10 min (business / marketing)

**[COLD OPEN - 0:00]**
Most visitors decide whether to stay on your website within a few seconds, and it's rarely the design that loses them. It's a headline that answers a question nobody asked.

**[PROMISE - 0:12]**
In this video I'll give you a three-line formula for the top of your homepage, show it applied to three real-world business types, and by the end you'll be able to rewrite yours in ten minutes.

**[SECTION 1 - The question every visitor is asking - 0:40]**
"Am I in the right place, and what do I get?" Your first screen must answer that before anything else.
*Open loop:* There's one word that kills more headlines than any other. I'll show it in section 3.

**[SECTION 2 - The formula - 2:00]**
- **Line 1, Outcome headline:** what the customer gets, in their words. ("Get your weekends back.")
- **Line 2, How + for whom:** ("Bookkeeping for freelancers, done in 15 minutes a month.")
- **Line 3, One clear action:** a single button with a specific verb. ("Start your free month.")

**[SECTION 3 - Before/after rewrites - 4:30]** *(payoff: the killer word is "solutions")*
1. Dental clinic - Before: "Innovative dental solutions." After: "Same-week appointments. Gentle care for nervous patients. Book online in 60 seconds."
2. SaaS tool - Before: "The all-in-one platform for teams." After: "Ship projects on time without the status meetings. Task tracking for agencies of 5-50. Try it free."
3. Local bakery - Before: "Welcome to our website." After: "Sourdough baked at 5 AM, on your table by 8. Order by midnight for morning delivery."

**[SECTION 4 - How to test it - 7:30]**
Show the page to five people for five seconds, then ask: "What do they sell, and who is it for?" If they can't answer, rewrite line 1.

**[OUTRO + CTA - 9:00]**
Grab the free fill-in-the-blank template in the description, rewrite your three lines, and post your before-and-after in the comments. I'll review the best ones in a future video.
